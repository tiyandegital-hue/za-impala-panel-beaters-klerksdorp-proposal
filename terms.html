Legal & Regulatory Documentation

Terms & Conditions

Workshop terms and collision repair agreement under the Consumer Protection Act (No. 68 of 2008) and the Competition Commission Automotive Aftermarket Guidelines.

Return to Quote Desk

1. Estimates are provisional until inspection

Any estimate given from photographs is provisional. The final quotation is confirmed only after a physical strip-and-quote inspection of your vehicle, when hidden damage can be seen and measured. If additional damage is found, we will contact you before proceeding.

2. Your right to choose your repairer

Under the South African Competition Commission Guidelines for the Automotive Aftermarket, you may choose your own repairer. Your insurer may suggest a repairer, but cannot force you to use one.

3. Authorisation and payment

Repair work begins only after you — or your insurer, on your instruction — have authorised the estimate. For insurance repairs, your insurer’s excess (if any) is set by your policy, not by the workshop, and is payable as your policy requires. For private repairs, payment terms are agreed in writing before work begins.

4. Parts

Where your vehicle’s manufacturer approval requires it, genuine OEM parts are used. Where an equivalent part is proposed, it will be identified to you and agreed in writing before fitment.

5. Vehicle custody

Your vehicle remains in our care from drop-off until collection. Vehicles are collected by the registered owner or by a person authorised by the owner in writing. Uncollected vehicles may attract storage charges — any such charge will be disclosed to you in writing before it applies.

6. Workmanship

Repair work is carried out to the manufacturer’s repair methods for your vehicle’s approval standard (Ford SA, Chery SA or Stellantis). Warranty terms for the repair itself are confirmed in writing with your estimate — no warranty period is promised on this website.

7. Disputes

If you are dissatisfied with any repair, contact Mandy Wolmarans directly on 018 462 8864 or at mandy@impalapanelbeaters.co.za so we can inspect the work together. Unresolved disputes may be referred to the Motor Industry Ombudsman of South Africa.
