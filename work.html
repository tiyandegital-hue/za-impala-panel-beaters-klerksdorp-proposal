Factory-Spec Recovery vs. Unregulated Repairs

Why proper reconstruction matters after a collision

When vehicle panels are bent, structural crumple zones and factory safety systems are compromised. A repair that only looks straight is not a repair.

01

Structural Geometry

Chassis jigs vs cosmetic patches

A chassis offset you cannot see can disrupt steering stability, wear tyres unevenly, and affect how safety systems respond in a second impact. Frame geometry is measured, not eyeballed.

02

Corrosion Prevention

E-coat primers & zinc sealants

Panel beating disturbs factory zinc coatings. Proper repair restores corrosion protection layer by layer — primer, sealer and paint — so the repair lasts as long as the car.

03

Resale & Warranty

OEM body integrity protection

Repairs outside manufacturer standards can affect your factory cover and your car’s resale value. Check your warranty terms, and keep the repair record from a certified workshop.
