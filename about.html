Why Certified Manufacturer Approval Matters

What approval actually buys you

01

Structural Crumple Zones

Frame geometry

Certified repairs use computerised laser alignment benches. A frame that is even slightly out can change how the car steers, brakes and protects you.

02

Anti-Corrosion Protection

Galvanic integrity

Factory collision repairs mandate multi-stage OEM e-coat application, structural seam sealers and cavity protection — not just paint over bare metal.

03

Resale & Factory Warranty

Resale preservation

A documented repair at an approved facility protects your car’s value and your position with the manufacturer. Keep the repair record.

04

ADAS & Radar Alignment

Digital sensor rig

Emergency braking, lane departure and blind-spot systems rely on radars and cameras embedded in bumpers and windscreens. They are checked and recalibrated after repair.
