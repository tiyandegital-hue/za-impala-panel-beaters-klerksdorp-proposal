3 tips for an accurate quotation

1Step 3 metres back for context

Capture the whole car in one shot. This reveals panel gap inconsistencies and shows the damage in context.

2Avoid flash & midday sun glare

Harsh reflections hide soft creases. Diffused shade or an overcast sky gives the truest view of the damage.

3Include wheel arches & rocker sills

Helps our technicians see whether suspension or steering geometry may have shifted.
