Legal & Regulatory Documentation

Privacy Policy

Statutory disclosure under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA No. 4 of 2013). This policy sets out how vehicle owner, fleet supervisor and driver information is collected, used and secured.

Return to Quote Desk

1. What information we collect

We collect only what is needed to assess your vehicle, prepare an estimate, and liaise with your insurer:

  • Driver and insured party data: full legal names, contact telephone numbers and email addresses, and driver’s licence confirmation where required for a claim.
  • Vehicle and collision data: vehicle identification number (VIN), registration plates, make, model and paint code, damage photographs, and South African Police Service (SAPS) accident references where you provide them.
  • Claim data: insurer name, policy details and claim numbers you supply for the purpose of your claim.

2. Why we collect it

Your information is used only for:

  • Collision assessment and estimating — preparing your repair estimate.
  • Insurance claim processing — sending authenticated quotes, photographs and repair updates to your insurer or broker, only with your instruction.
  • Repair records — keeping a record of the work done on your vehicle, as required by the Consumer Protection Act.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

We share your information only where necessary to complete your repair or claim:

  • Your insurance underwriter or broker, for claim processing and clearance.
  • Authorised parts suppliers for Ford SA, Chery SA and Stellantis, to source correct OEM parts.
  • Statutory authorities such as the South African Police Service, where the law requires it.

4. Your rights under POPIA

Under sections 23 to 25 of POPIA, you may:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct outdated contact numbers, policy details or vehicle registration details.
  • Objection — object to how your information is handled, on reasonable grounds.
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator if you are dissatisfied with our handling of your information.

5. How to contact us about your data

Formal POPIA enquiries, access requests or deletion requests should be addressed to our Information Officer, Mandy Wolmarans, at mandy@impalapanelbeaters.co.za or by telephone on 018 462 8864.
