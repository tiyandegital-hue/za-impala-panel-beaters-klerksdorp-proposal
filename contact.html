Emergency Protocol

What to do at the scene of an accident

If you have just experienced a vehicle collision in Klerksdorp or anywhere along North West roadways. Need immediate advice? Call 018 462 8864.

01

Priority: Life Safety

Stop & ensure physical safety

Switch on hazard lights immediately. Position the emergency warning triangle a safe distance behind your vehicle. Move everyone to safety before anything else.

02

Priority: Evidence Record

Document scene & drivers

Take comprehensive photos before vehicles are moved: wide angles of the road, the damage, licence discs and the other driver’s details.

03

Priority: Legal Compliance

Obtain SAPS case number

Under South African law, road traffic collisions must be officially reported to the nearest South African Police Service station.

04

Right to Choose Repairer

Notify insurer & nominate

Notify your insurer to register your collision claim. Remember your consumer right: you may choose your own repairer.

05

Recovery Destination

Towing caution & drop-off

Do not allow an unauthorised roadside tow truck to take your vehicle without explicit approval. Confirm the drop-off destination with your insurer first.
