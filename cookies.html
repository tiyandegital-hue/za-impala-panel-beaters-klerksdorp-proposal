Cookie Policy

This website uses functional session cookies and local browser storage only. There is no behavioural tracking, no advertising tracking, and no cross-site tracking.

1. What this site stores

  • Functional session data only: the quote form and the insurance checklist keep your progress in your own browser while you use the page, so you do not lose what you typed if you move between steps.
  • No tracking cookies: this site does not set advertising cookies, analytics cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No third-party embeds: fonts are hosted on this website. No maps, videos or social-media embeds load from third parties on this site.

2. What we do not do

We do not build advertising profiles, do not sell browsing data, and do not share your browsing behaviour with any third party. The quote form and contact form send the details you type directly to the workshop desk — nothing else is recorded about your visit.

3. Managing cookies and storage

You can clear this site’s stored data at any time from your browser’s settings (usually under Privacy → Clear browsing data). Clearing it will reset the quote form and checklist progress on your device only.

4. Questions
