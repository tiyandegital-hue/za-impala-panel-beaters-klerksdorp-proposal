Climate-Controlled Finishing

Spray painting & computerised colour matching

Multi-angle digital spectrophotometer formula scanning to counteract natural UV fading, so the new paint blends with the rest of the car. Finished in a pressurised downdraft low-bake spray booth that keeps dust and overspray out of the finish.

What to do first: to speed up your quotation, photograph the alphanumeric paint code stamped on your vehicle’s manufacturer plate and include it with your quote request.

04

Non-Invasive Metallurgy

Paintless dent removal (PDR) & metal smoothing

Surgical massage of outer sheet metal from the reverse panel face, using specialised tools — no filler, no respray, factory clear coat preserved. Applicable strictly where the factory clear coat has sustained no fracturing, cracking or edge peeling.

Ideal for: shopping mall car park door dings, shallow shopping trolley creases, falling branch or pebble indentations.

What to do first: send clear photos of each dent through the quote form so we can confirm PDR is suitable before you drive in.

05

Foundational Integrity // Jig Ops

Chassis straightening & frame realignment

Computer-controlled hydraulic frame-bench tensioning with laser datum measurement. Mounting platforms are re-centred to factory pitch and yaw, and full wheel geometry is checked so the car tracks straight without pulling.

Why it matters: modern vehicles depend on precise frame geometry for proper airbag deployment and sensor alignment. A bent frame is a safety issue, not a cosmetic one.

What to do first: if your car pulls to one side, the steering wheel sits off-centre, or a wheel rubs after a knock, bring it in for a frame inspection.
